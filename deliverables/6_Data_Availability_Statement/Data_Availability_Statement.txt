DATA AVAILABILITY STATEMENT

Manuscript: Resilient Data Science Programming for Fault-Tolerant Distributed Analytics in Multi-Cluster Spark Environments
Team ID: 26FSPDSCPMID4098 (MACSE502)

The datasets generated and analysed during the current study are available in the GitHub repository, https://github.com/k745698/rmc-spark: the raw experiment measurements (run records, decision logs, cluster time series and stage events) are in the results/raw directory, and the synthetic taxi-trip and graph datasets are regenerated deterministically by the seeded scripts in jobs/ and scripts/. The public datasets analysed in the case studies are available from the UCI Machine Learning Repository (AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance Dataset, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5HS5C) and from the Machine Learning Group of ULB via Kaggle (Credit Card Fraud Detection, https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mlg-ulb/creditcardfraud); the latter contains only anonymised, PCA-transformed transaction features. The optional NYC TLC Trip Record Data (https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page) can be downloaded with scripts/download_data.py.
